# Hagen

- **Género:** masculino. Dagda: *He turns his back on his enemy twice, yet calls himself a warrior?* (event/e647/e647.mbm#13); la opción del jugador dice *Tell him about the quest* (e647#8).
- **Trato (propuesta, falta la aprobación del usuario; E-30):**
  - **Tú** con Nanashi, de guerrero veterano a joven. Lo llama **child** (*Stop, child.*, e647#0; *Halt, child...*, e647#1; *Child, I shall teach you...*, e647#5), **boy** (*Well done, boy.*, battle/event/BtlEventSub147/BtlEventSub147.mbm#1) y, cuando lo respeta, **noble child** y **noble warrior** (BtlEventSub147#2, #7). Nanashi es masculino según el juego (*boy*). Nunca *vosotros* (E-11, E-31).
  - A quien sirve lo nombra *my king* (e647#9, #10) y, en la negociación, *my Majesty* / *Your Majesty* (battle/talk/TalkToneBoss62.mbm#1, #19, #21).
- **Registro:** de época, caballeresco y solemne (E-31, pendiente: aquí solo se describe). No usa *thou*, pero sí fórmulas antiguas y de juramento:
  - *Halt*, *Hear me.* (e647#1, #9); *I shall teach you the difference between bravery and recklessness. Now, draw your blade.* (e647#5).
  - *I, Hagen, vow that I will never reveal the location of the treasure!* (BtlEventSub147#3).
  - *Joy has always led to sorrow. Come, you who are fated to die. You shall meet your end here!* (BtlEventSub147#6).
  - *follow me to the ends of the Earth!* (e647#10); *Let us fight to our heart's content!* (e647#14).
  - Honor y caballería: *the honorable way to fight* (e647#5); *This goes against the code of chivalry!* (TalkToneBoss62#24).
  - Mezcla con frases modernas y llanas (*I'm not a fan of picking a fight with a child*, BtlEventSub147#0).
- **Muletillas y tics:**
  - Repite su deber: *I need only carry out my king's orders... And ensure the gold never falls into the wrong hands.* (e647#9 y #10, casi idénticas).
  - *I have no choice* (BtlEventSub147#0, #5).
  - Interjecciones: *Hm...* (e647#1), *Hmph...* (e647#9), *Ngh...* (BtlEventSub147#5), *Heheh...* (e647#14).
- **Groserías:** ninguna. Desprecio de caballero: *A thief.* (e647#9), *Bandit* (TalkToneBoss62#18, #25), *How cowardly...!* (TalkToneBoss62#24).
