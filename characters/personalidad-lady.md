# Personalidad: Lady (TalkToneLady)

- **Género:** no se marca (E-41). La voz se llama *Lady*, pero la línea tiene que poder decirla un demonio de cualquier género. Aquí el inglés sí marca a quien habla en varias líneas:
  - *You think I'm a foolish lady, don't you?* (battle/talk/TalkToneLady.mbm#17); *I'm a lady! Please don't bring up money...* (#18); *Human ladies must have it worse...* (#19); *You shouldn't make a lady shed tears...* (#208); *You cannot touch a lady's hand* (#244); *Having another lady...* (#251); *You don't know what a mature lady is, do you?* (#295); *If you take it too easy, a lady's mind will change.* (#305); *trying to lure in a lady with money* (#310); *Ladies are suckers for gifts. ... the person she loves.* (#311); *”My Queen” you say? ... I am a still a princess.* (#316); *You need to learn how to flirt with women...* (#281); *Are you always this forceful when you flirt with women?* (#303).
  - En las opciones y la narración: *Stop her*, *Say something to her* (#110, #119), *Gaze at her intensely* (#150), *Laugh with her* (#156), *Go near her* (#200), *Give her a blue rose* (#232), *Psychically scout her* (#332).
  - Según DECISIONS 2026-11-09, se redacta sin marcar el género cuando se puede; si la línea lo exige (*lady*, *princess*, *Queen*), va a delicadas y a PENDING. Queda pendiente para el usuario.
- **Trato:** propuesta, pendiente de aprobación (E-30): **tú** con el jugador. Habla con cortesía refinada y a veces lo llama **sir** (*Good sir, could you please give me that?*, #41; *You are late, sir.*, #71), pero lo trata desde arriba y con coquetería: **boy** (*Why, you're still a boy...*, #16; *a bad boy*, #23), **child** (*Child, come back when you've become an adult.*, #300), **young man** (#58, #69), **handsome** (#12), **slave** (#250, #314, #317). Con tanta coquetería y condescendencia, el *usted* sonaría frío; si el usuario prefiere marcar la cortesía, la alternativa es **usted** en toda la voz (E-30: el trato se mantiene).
- **Registro:** refinado, seductor y peligroso: cortesía de dama (*Would you be so kind as to do me a favor?*, #5; *Such a pleasure to meet you.*, #7; *Au revoir...*, #65), pocas contracciones cuando se pone solemne (*I do not wish to involve myself with you...*, #131; *Let us shake hands*, #239), y amenazas dichas con la misma dulzura (*Swear your eternal love to me in my stomach!*, #171; *I will stuff that lovely smile as a trophy!*, #291). Coquetea sin parar (*Hello, there, handsome...*, #12; *You can touch me, if you want?*, #161) y se dice dominante (*I like looking down on others*, #153).
  - De época (E-31): leve. *shall* (#111, #129, #163), *Let us* (#1, #178), *I grow weary of this* (#63), *Do not touch me* (#242). Cortesía moderna, sin arcaísmos.
- **Muletillas y tics:**
  - *My...* / *My,* para abrir (#8, #9, #64, #66, #137, #163, #167, #258, #266, #280); *My, my.* (#286); *you hear?* al final (#9, #59); *Aren't we being shy...* (#54); *Is that so...* (#56, #60, #61); *please* en los pedidos (#33-39).
  - Risas: *Hehe...* (#149, #196, #203, #213, #285, #305), *Hehe... Hehehe...* (#155).
  - Exclamaciones: *Oooaaahhhh...! Oh, oh, oh...?/Yooouuu... can have this... I give!* (#27); *Ahh, I've finally met a man...* (#228); *Ahhh...* (#296); *P-Please...* (#173) (E-55); *I'm... I'm so embarassed...* (#296).
- **Groserías:** ninguna. Insultos refinados: *despicable human* (#2), *good-for-nothing summoner* (#22), *You digusting pig!* (#221), *You coward.* (#229, #329), *You are nothing but a dog wagging its tail.* (#63), *Your stupidity is beyond salvation!* (#138). Igualar la intensidad (E-22).
- **Fórmulas comunes:** mismo índice que en [personalidad-boy.md](personalidad-boy.md) para #1-74. En esta voz:
  - Aceptar unirse: *...You have shown me your sincerity.* (#4); *I'm your ally, too. Don't you forget that.* (#14); *I'll become your ally.* (#67); súplica *Please, spare me... I'll beome your ally./So please, let's talk?* (#115, #125; la segunda termina en *talk...?*).
  - Presentación: *I'm <raza> <nombre>. Such a pleasure to meet you.* (#7).
  - Pedir: siempre *I would like...*: *I would like {objeto} x{cantidad}...* (#30), *I would like {cantidad} Macca...* (#31), *I would like the life of your ally... {demonio}...* (#40), *I would like the HP of your partner... Yes, {aliado}'s HP...* (#42), *Good sir, could you please give me that?* (#41). Cerrar con macca: *I want to feel more of your passion.../{cantidad} Macca, and I'm yours.* (#29).
  - PS y PM en cuatro grados, con *please*: *I would like your HP... Just a little. / Some of it, please. / A good portion of it, please. / An absurd amount, please!* (#32-35; MP, #36-39).
- **Trampas de género (E-41):** además de las líneas marcadas arriba: *I'll become your ally* (#67, #115) y *I'm your ally, too* (#14): con verbo («me uno a ti», «yo también estoy de tu lado»). *I'm satisfied* (#55), *I cannot move* (#24, neutro), *I'm disappointed* (#300), *I'm quite jealous* (#322), *I'm now full* (#345), *I'm so embarassed* (#296), *I'm in a terrible hurry* (#181), *I'm sorry* (neutro), *little old me* (#167), *you're being fooled by me* (#322): sin adjetivos con género para quien habla («¡Qué decepción!», «Con esto me basta»).
